# v0.4.6 — verified Antigravity ACP opt-in

v0.4.6 turns the previously informational Google Antigravity provider row into
an actionable, deliberately consented ACP connection while preserving a strict
third-party trust boundary.

## Antigravity connection

- Selecting **Google Antigravity (community bridge)** in `/provider` now opens
  a host-owned confirmation before any download or process launch.
- The confirmation states that the bridge is not provided or endorsed by
  Google and repeats the maintainer's warning that third-party Antigravity
  OAuth use may violate Google's Terms and risk account suspension.
- On explicit confirmation, Codypendent downloads the pinned
  `antigravity-acp` v1.0.0 binary for the current macOS or Linux architecture,
  verifies its immutable SHA-256 digest, installs it privately, performs a real
  ACP handshake, and opens the models actually advertised by the bridge.
- An operator-installed `agy-acp` or `antigravity-acp` executable on `PATH`
  continues to take precedence and is never replaced automatically.
- Codypendent does not read or store Antigravity credentials. Authentication
  remains owned by Antigravity and the bridge process.

The non-interactive equivalent is intentionally explicit:

```sh
codypendent acp connect antigravity --accept-community-risk
```

The same acknowledgement is required for `acp install` and `acp probe`.
Opening the provider catalogue, listing ACP clients, or declining the TUI
confirmation never downloads or launches the bridge.

## Upgrade

```sh
codypendent update v0.4.6
```

Or use the installer:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CodeHalwell/codypendent/main/install.sh | bash -s -- v0.4.6
```
